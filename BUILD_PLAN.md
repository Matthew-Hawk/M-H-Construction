# First website build

## Scope and visual direction

Build a local, responsive Astro + TypeScript preview for M&H Construction with the owner's horizontal navy logo, warm ivory backgrounds, generous interior imagery, restrained serif headings, and clear kitchen/bathroom inquiry paths.

Include Home, Kitchens, Bathrooms, Our Work, About, Process, Service Areas, and Contact. The portfolio will explicitly state that its AI-generated images are visual placeholders, with no fictitious project facts or before/after comparisons. Company history, team biographies, testimonials, credentials, and detailed capabilities remain pending owner input.

## Architecture

Static HTML output, shared layouts, centralized business/service content, CSS variables, and small scripts for the mobile menu and email inquiry preparation. No CMS, database, tracking, or collection backend in this preview. The contact form prepares an email in the visitor's own email app, explicitly states this behavior, and never claims delivery.

Production is intended for Hostinger, subject to confirmation of Business Web Hosting versus a Builder-managed website. Registration/hosting/DNS changes and production launch are separate from this local preview and require owner authorization. No Sites hosting registration is needed for the requested Hostinger workflow.

## Content and launch dependencies

- Replace generated placeholders with approved real project photos before launch.
- Confirm exact Hostinger plan and migration access.
- Confirm specific capabilities, business background, and any claims before publication.
- Choose secure inquiry provider/backend, validate delivery and failure behavior, and define retention/deletion.
- Draft the production privacy notice against actual hosting/form data flows; legal review as appropriate.
- Confirm existing URLs, redirects, backup, and recovery before deployment.
- Analytics remains off unless authorized.

## Milestones

1. First local visual preview and working navigation/contact alternatives.
2. Owner design/copy feedback and real content replacement.
3. Hostinger architecture confirmation, secure lead handling, and accurate privacy notice.
4. Mobile/accessibility/SEO verification, backup and redirect review.
5. Explicitly authorized production launch and rollback documentation.
